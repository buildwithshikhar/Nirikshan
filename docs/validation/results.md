## Scenario descriptions

- `clean_live`: Three adjacent recordings with distinct parameter sets, zero padding at both ends.
- `deleted_intact_zero`: One live and two deleted-but-intact clips, 4 KiB-aligned zero padding, noise outside.
- `zero_gaps`: Gaps of 1 B to 64 KiB of zeros between clips.
- `zero_pad_inside_narrow`: Zero bytes between NAL units within the default 64-byte limit.
- `zero_pad_inside_wide`: Padding beyond max_pad: documented limitation, expected to lose data.
- `random_gaps_between`: 100-5000 bytes of random data (no zero runs) after each clip; absorbed into the last NAL.
- `random_gaps_inside`: 50-600 random bytes inserted between NAL units, twice per clip.
- `partial_overwrite_zero`: Head/middle/tail 20-60% of a deleted clip zeroed; survivors scored on recoverable frames.
- `partial_overwrite_foreign`: Region replaced by the start of another clip (itself a truth clip).
- `fully_overwritten`: One intact survivor plus a clip fully overwritten (zeros, noise or another clip); the destroyed clip must not appear.
- `fragmented_zero_join_off`: Clip in 2-4 NAL-aligned fragments with zero gaps.
- `fragmented_zero_join_on`: Same images as above with the heuristic reassembler enabled.
- `fragmented_decoy_join_off`: A1, other clip B, A2.
- `fragmented_decoy_join_on`: Same images; every join candidate is a wrong one (false-accept study).
- `adversarial_noise_with_clips`: False start codes, fake H.264/H.265 parameter-set groups and fake IDRs around two real clips.
- `multi_channel_gop`: 2-3 cameras, whole GOPs alternate. No channel metadata: demultiplexing is out of scope for the generic carver.
- `multi_channel_frame`: 2-3 cameras interleaved per frame. Expected to fail for the generic carver.
- `neg_noise_start_codes`: No real video; must yield no decodable clip.
- `neg_encrypted_whole`: Looks random; must yield nothing.
- `neg_encrypted_slices_params_clear`: Headers and parameter sets readable, slices encrypted.
- `neg_encrypted_all_payloads`: Start codes and NAL headers clear.
- `neg_mjpeg`: Not NAL-based; must yield nothing.
- `neg_mpeg4`: Different start-code scheme; must yield nothing.
- `clean_live@dhav[generic]`: Three adjacent recordings with distinct parameter sets, zero padding at both ends. Frames wrapped in DHAV headers/trailers per dhav.c.
